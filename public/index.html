01 / PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your inbox is not a prompt to a server.

There is no app backend or analytics. Messages stay in this tab until you export them. Model and runtime downloads still contact their hosts; using your browser may create normal network logs.

02 / HONEST ABOUT UNCERTAINTY

A close match is not a certainty.

Similarity scores are not probabilities. Weak or ambiguous matches go to “Needs review.” Every suggestion shows its closest example, and you can change any category.

03 / KEEP THE HUMAN IN THE LOOP

Organise first. Reply yourself.

ReplyQueue never sends messages, approves refunds, or changes orders. It suggests a next step for you to check. The sample inbox is synthetic, not customer data.
